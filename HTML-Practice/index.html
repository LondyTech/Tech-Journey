About Me

My name is Londiwe Myeza, born and raised in Durban, South Africa, and I am currently pursuing my Bcom in Accounting at the University of KwaZulu-Natal (UKZN). When I was accepted into UKZN, I had to choose between Accounting and Computer Science. Honestly, I chose Accounting because of the career opportunities and, after doing some research, I was relieved to hear there was less mathematics, especially coming from a Mathematics, Physical Sciences, and Chemistry background. However, as I progressed, I realised that although I could do Accounting, I did not enjoy its repetitive nature. Ironically, the modules I enjoyed most were Information Systems and Technology, Statistics, and Machine Learning, where I not only performed well but also found myself helping other students with their computer skills. That experience made me realise that I may have chosen the practical path, but technology was where my interest really was.


My interest in technology is not something I discovered overnight. Growing up, I enjoyed building LEGO, solving puzzles, playing games, and figuring out how things worked. I have always been hands-on, curious, and creative, and I enjoy turning ideas into something real. After being financially excluded from university in 2026 due to historical debt, I had to step back and rethink my direction. Instead of seeing this as the end of my journey, I decided to use it as an opportunity to pursue something I genuinely enjoy. Software development combines creativity, problem-solving, logic, and continuous learning all qualities that feel natural to me. In a way, getting lost in Accounting helped me find my way back to technology.


Over the next year, my goal is to become job-ready in software development by consistently learning, practising, and building real-world projects. I know the tech industry is constantly changing, so I want to develop the ability to learn, adapt, and grow with it rather than simply learn one skill and stop. I aim to build a strong portfolio, secure my first opportunity in the industry, and grow into a confident full-stack developer, with the long-term goal of venturing into software engineering. I want to build things that are useful, understand the code I write, and let my work speak for itself. After all, I may have started with Accounting because of the money but, I'm choosing software development because I actually enjoy the journey.


In addition to my technical skills, I am also focused on developing my soft skills, such as communication, teamwork, and adaptability. I believe that being a well-rounded developer involves not only writing code but also effectively collaborating with others and understanding the needs of users. I am excited about the journey ahead and am determined to make the most of every opportunity to learn and grow in this dynamic field.
