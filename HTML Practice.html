About Me

My name is Londiwe Myeza, born and raised in Durban, South Africa. I am a Bachelor of Commerce in Accounting student at the University of KwaZulu-Natal (UKZN). Unfortunately, I was financially excluded in 2026 because I was unable to settle my historical debt before registration. Although this setback interrupted my studies, it gave me the opportunity to reflect on my career aspirations and pursue a path that truly aligns with my interests and strengths.

When I was accepted into university, I had to choose between studying Accounting and Computer Science. After doing my research, I decided on Accounting because it seemed to offer more stable career opportunities for someone without a degree and appeared to involve less mathematics. However, as I progressed through the programme, I realised that while I was capable of doing the work, I was not passionate about it. I found the repetitive nature of accounting unfulfilling. During my time away from university, I reconnected with something I had always loved technology and problem-solving. Ever since I was young, I have enjoyed building, creating, and figuring out how things work. I've always been a hands-on, curious person who enjoys turning ideas into reality. Software development combines creativity, logic, and continuous learning, making it the perfect career for me. It is not just a career choice but something I genuinely enjoy and can see myself doing for many years.

Over the next year, my goal is to build a strong foundation in software development by consistently learning, practising, and creating real world projects. I want to develop the technical and problem-solving skills that will make me job ready and allow me to secure my first role as a software developer. More importantly, I want to become a developer who understands the technologies I work with and writes clean, meaningful code that speaks for itself. I am committed to continuous learning and personal growth, and I look forward to building a career where I can make a positive impact through technology.

In addition to my technical skills, I am also focused on developing my soft skills, such as communication, teamwork, and adaptability. I believe that being a well-rounded developer involves not only writing code but also effectively collaborating with others and understanding the needs of users. I am excited about the journey ahead and am determined to make the most of every opportunity to learn and grow in this dynamic field.
